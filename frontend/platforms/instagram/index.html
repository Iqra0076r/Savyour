Download Instagram videos

Copy the link to an individual Instagram Reel or video post, paste it into Savyour, review the thumbnail and available formats, then save your permitted video. Example link: instagram.com/reel or instagram.com/p. Instagram availability depends on the source. Choose the best available quality when offered.

How to save a Instagram video

Open the public Reel or video post itself and copy its link, usually under /reel/ or /p/. An Instagram profile link does not select a single video. Savyour checks the post and shows the thumbnail and available quality choices when the source permits access. A post may look public in your signed-in Instagram app but still require an account when opened by the server. If the thumbnail appears and saving fails, the preview may have been accessible while the video stream was not. Try the original post URL and confirm it opens in a private browser window without signing in. Stories, private accounts and access restricted posts are outside this public-link flow.

Common issue

Instagram may return post metadata while requiring sign-in for the actual video stream.
