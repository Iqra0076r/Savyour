Copy the link to an individual Facebook video post, paste it into Savyour, review the thumbnail and available formats, then save your permitted video. Example link: facebook.com or fb.watch video. Facebook availability depends on the source. Choose the best available quality when offered.

How to save a Facebook video

Copy a direct link to a public Facebook video or use its fb.watch share link. A feed, group or profile URL does not identify a specific playable post. When the post is accessible, Savyour displays the preview and the formats returned by the source. Audience settings matter: videos shared only with friends, members of a group or signed-in viewers can fail even when you personally see them in Facebook. If analysis cannot find media, open the link while signed out and confirm the video itself is public. The best available quality varies with the post and its accessible streams; the site cannot raise the original resolution.
