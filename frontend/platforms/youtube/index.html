Download YouTube videos

Copy the link to an individual YouTube video or Short, paste it into Savyour, review the thumbnail and available formats, then save your permitted video. Example link: youtube.com/watch or youtube.com/shorts. YouTube availability depends on the source. YouTube video quality is limited to 480p.

How to save a YouTube video

  1. Copy the link to an individual public YouTube video or Short.
  2. Paste the youtube.com/watch or youtube.com/shorts link into the YouTube downloader and check the preview.
  3. Select up to 480p quality and save permitted media.

Supported YouTube links

Individual public videos and Shorts may work when their media streams are publicly available. Private and login-restricted posts may fail. A thumbnail alone does not guarantee a downloadable file.

Permissions

Only download media you own or have permission to save.
